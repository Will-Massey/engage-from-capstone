Questions about Engage

How does the free trial work?

Seven days, no card, the full product. Look a real prospect up, send a real proposal, and judge Engage on whether the letter comes back signed.

Is there a contract?

Monthly, cancel anytime. The price is on this page, flat for the whole team, which is not how this category usually behaves.

How does the e-signing work?

The engagement letter goes out with the proposal and the client signs on any device. Every signature carries a forensic audit trail: who signed, when, and against exactly which version of the document. If it is ever questioned, the record answers.

What does the AML onboarding actually cover?

Your client uploads their ID documents through Engage and they land in a practice-side review queue for you to approve. It handles the collection and the paper trail. Your AML policy and your MLRO judgement stay yours.
